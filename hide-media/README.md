# AniList Hide Media

by **YoruC**

For the chat of [AniList DMs](../dms): every picture, GIF, video and YouTube clip other people send is folded away,
like a spoiler, until you click it. In its place you see a small button: **Image hidden, click to view**.

- Works on normal and live messages in the DMs chat (you need AniList DMs installed too)
- Click the button to see the picture; a tiny **hide** button folds it away again
- Your own messages and AniList's own spoilers are left alone
- Hidden pictures are not loaded until you open them
- Pictures in posts on AniList itself are not touched, unless you switch that on in the options
- PC and Firefox for Android

## Install

1. Install the **Tampermonkey** extension for your browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge or Firefox).
2. Open **[anilist-hide-media.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YoruCAnime/Anilist/refs/heads/main/hide-media/anilist-hide-media.user.js)**
   and click **Install** on Tampermonkey's page.
3. Reload your AniList tabs.

Updates arrive by themselves through Tampermonkey.

## Options

Tampermonkey icon > AniList Hide Media:

- **Hiding on / off**
- **Hide my own messages too: on / off**
- **On the site too**: also fold away pictures in posts on AniList (feed, replies, forum, bios, reviews)
